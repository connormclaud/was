# Fueling & Timing Protocols

To support athletic performance, preserve neural adaptations, and ensure glycogen replenishment without compromising fat loss, nutrient timing and workout fueling follow strict protocols.

## Strength Peri-Workout Fueling (Tuesday & Thursday)

Caloric deficits must never be created around the lifting window. Fueling is structured to protect performance in 3×5 lifts and promote rapid post-exercise recovery.

| Timing | Target | Nutrient Source | Purpose |
|--------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| **45–60 min Pre-Workout** | 30–40 g carbohydrates | Fast-acting carbs (e.g., banana, oats) | Top off liver/muscle glycogen, enhance lifting performance |
| **Immediate Post-Workout** | 30–40 g protein | Whole protein (e.g., Skyr, chicken, protein source) | Halt catabolism, initiate muscle protein synthesis (MPS) |

### Daily Deficit Adjustment
On strength training days, calories are restricted from **evening fats** and **daytime incidental snacking** rather than the peri-workout fueling window.

## Saturday On-Bike Fueling (Long Ride)

Deficits must not be created on the bike. Under-fueling long rides leads to glycogen depletion, compromised Sunday recovery, and post-ride rebound binging.

| Parameter | Target / Protocol |
|-----------|-------------------|
| **Intake Rate** | 40–60 g carbohydrates per hour during the ride |
| **Carbohydrate Sources** | Energy gels, chews, bananas, or high-carb liquid mix |
| **Total Daily Expenditure Adjustment** | Daily intake scales dynamically with Garmin recorded burn |

### Example Daily Balance
- **Garmin Recorded Ride Burn**: ~1,800 kcal
- **Total Daily Burn**: ~4,100 kcal
- **Target Intake**: ~3,600 kcal total for Saturday

## Pre-Breakfast Run Protocol (Monday & Friday)

On mornings scheduled for a 20-minute fasted Zone 2 jog, the protocol protects against dehydration and muscle breakdown:

1. **Pre-Run Hydration**: Drink 300–500 ml of water with a pinch of salt prior to stepping out.
2. **Post-Run Nutrition**: Eat the standard high-protein breakfast (Skyr + Magerquark bowl, ~65 g protein) **immediately** after finishing the run to halt muscle protein breakdown.
