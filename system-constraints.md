# System Constraints

The nutrition strategy is calibrated for a specific baseline physiological profile, establishing strict energy budgets and macronutrient floors to achieve steady fat loss while preserving skeletal muscle mass.

## Baseline Physiological Profile

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Body Weight | 100 kg | Total mass |
| Skeletal Muscle Mass (SMM) | ~38.0 kg | Target mass for preservation |
| Body Fat | ~28.6% | Fat-free mass = ~71.4 kg |
| Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) | ~1,850 kcal/day | Baseline cellular requirement |
| Non-Exercise Baseline | ~2,310 kcal/day | Daily baseline expenditure without training |

## Energy Budget & Weekly Deficit Architecture

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Weekly Cumulative Deficit | ~3,250–3,500 kcal/week |
| Estimated Weekly Fat Loss | ~0.42–0.45 kg/week |
| Sunday Energy Budget | ~2,300 kcal (Full Maintenance) |
| High-Expenditure Days | Deficit-loaded days |

### Strategy

Rather than enforcing a flat daily caloric deficit, the weekly deficit is dynamically distributed across high-expenditure days. Sunday is maintained at full baseline (~2,300 kcal/day) to prevent feelings of restriction during batch-cooking and meal preparation.

## Fixed Macronutrient & Allocation Rules

### 1. Protein Floor (Non-Negotiable)
- **Target**: 160–180 g/day (~1.65–1.8 g/kg total body weight, or ~2.3 g/kg fat-free mass) across all 7 days.
- **Purpose**: Preserves 38 kg of skeletal muscle mass during caloric deficit.

### 2. Fat Floor
- **Target**: 65–75 g/day (~0.7 g/kg total body weight).
- **Sources**: Egg yolks, olive oil, nuts, and fatty fish (salmon, mackerel).
- **Purpose**: Supports hormonal health, joint integrity, and fat-soluble vitamin absorption.

### 3. Carbohydrate Allocation
- **Target**: All remaining calories beyond protein and fat floors.
- **Purpose**: Surplus calories above protein and fat floors are allocated entirely to carbohydrates to maintain muscle glycogen and fuel physical demands.

## Dietary Guardrails

- **Fiber**: ≥30 g/day from vegetables, berries, oats, rice, quinoa, and legumes.
- **Salt**: <5 g/day, using measured iodized salt applied during batch cooking.
- **Free Sugars**: <10% of total daily energy intake.
- **Plant Diversity**: ≥8 unique plant sources per day.
- **Micronutrient & Polyphenol Focus**: Dark chocolate (85%+), frozen berries in daily breakfast bowls.
