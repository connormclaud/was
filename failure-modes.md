# Failure Modes & Mitigations

System failures jeopardize compliance and recovery. Pre-defined mitigation protocols ensure rapid recovery without compromising macro targets or training adaptation.

| Failure Mode | Trigger / Risk | Mitigation Protocol |
|--------------|----------------|---------------------|
| **1. Forgot to Thaw Meal** | Frozen portion not moved from freezer night before | Microwaved defrost mode for 3 min + quick steam re-heat, or switch to emergency pantry backup (canned tuna/mackerel + instant rice). |
| **2. Missed Batch Cook Session** | Sunday or Wednesday session skipped due to schedule | **Emergency Fast Prep (10 min)**: Scramble 4 eggs + 1 tin drained tuna + microwaved frozen green beans + instant couscous/oats. |
| **3. On-Bike Fueling Deficit** | Forgot or ran out of carbs during Saturday long ride | Immediately consume 30–40 g fast carbs post-ride (e.g. banana + fruit juice) to protect Sunday recovery and prevent post-ride binge eating. |
| **4. Dry / Tough Batch Protein** | Chicken breast overcooked during batch session | Shred protein and fold in 50 g Skyr or Magerquark with a squeeze of lemon juice to restore moisture and texture. |
| **5. Severe Evening Hunger** | High-burn deficit day causing late-night craving | Consume 150 g Skyr/Magerquark or 2 hard-boiled egg whites with a large glass of salted water. Keeps additional intake strictly high-protein with minimal fat/carbs. |

---

## Unthawed Freezer Portion Protocol

If a Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday meal was not moved to the fridge the night before:
1. **Submerge Sealed Container**: Place the sealed container in a bowl of cold tap water for 15 minutes.
2. **Defrost Cycle**: Microwave at 30% power for 3 minutes, then heat at standard high power for 2 minutes.
3. **Pantry Swap**: Alternatively, keep canned tuna/mackerel and pre-cooked vacuum-sealed rice packets as a zero-cooking fallback.

---

## Missed Batch Cook Protocol

If a batch session cannot occur:
- **Protein**: Canned tuna, canned mackerel, Skyr/Magerquark, or eggs.
- **Carbohydrate**: Instant couscous or microwave rice pouches.
- **Micro/Fiber**: Frozen vegetables (steamed in microwave for 3 min).
