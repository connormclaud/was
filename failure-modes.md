# Failure Modes & Mitigations

Every system fails. The key is having a known recovery path.

| Failure Mode | Mitigation |
|--------------|------------|
| Missed prep session | Eggs + frozen greens + instant grain |
| Dry protein | Greek yogurt (1.5%) + acid/lemon rescue |
| Appetite suppression | Convert assembly to soup format (add hot broth) |

## Missed Prep

If Sunday or Wednesday prep doesn't happen, the emergency fallback is:

1. Scramble or fry eggs (protein)
2. Steam frozen greens (fiber, micronutrients)
3. Instant grain — couscous or quick oats (carbs)

This takes ~10 minutes and covers the [system constraints]({% link system-constraints.md %}) adequately.

## Dry Protein

Chicken that has dried out during storage can be rescued:

- Shred it and mix with Greek yogurt (1.5% fat)
- Add acid (lemon juice or ACV) to brighten and tenderize
- The result works well as a wrap or bowl filling

## Appetite Suppression

When a cold assembled meal isn't appealing:

- Heat broth (stock cube + hot water)
- Add the assembled components to the broth
- The warmth and liquid volume make it easier to eat

This converts any assembly into a soup without additional cooking.
